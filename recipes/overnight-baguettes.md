# Overnight Baguettes

**Yield / Target**

- 12
- 3 baguettes

## Ingredients

- King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour: 315 g
- King Arthur Whole Wheat Flour: 78 g
- water: 325 g
- table salt: 9 g
- instant yeast: 1 g
- sourdough starter, fed within the last 24 hours: 24 g

## Process

1. Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl and stir until mostly homogeneous. Use a bowl scraper to press and smear the dough until slightly smooth.
2. Transfer dough to a lightly greased container. Keep dough between 23°C and 26°C, cover, and rest 30 minutes.
3. Perform a bowl fold: stretch and fold the dough 8 to 12 times around the bowl. Turn seam-side down, cover, and rest 30 minutes.
4. Mark the dough height, then refrigerate to rise for 8 to 24 hours.
5. Warm dough at room temperature for 1 to 2 hours, until it has risen 1 1/2 to 2 times in volume.
6. Dust dough with whole wheat flour, turn it onto a lightly floured surface, and divide into 3 equal pieces, about 250 g each.
7. Pat each piece into a rough 6-inch square. Fold and seal into a cylinder, seam-side down. Repeat, cover, and rest 15 minutes.
8. Shape each piece into a baguette, tapering the ends, about 16 to 18 inches long.
9. Place baguettes seam-side up on a baker’s couche or stiff cotton towel dusted with whole wheat flour. Cover and proof until slightly puffy, about 45 to 60 minutes at about 20°C; proofing may take as few as 30 minutes in a warm environment.
10. Preheat oven to 260°C with a cast iron pan on the bottom or lowest rack and a baking steel or stone on the middle rack.
11. Roll proofed baguettes seam-side down onto parchment. Chill 10 minutes for easier scoring, if desired.
12. Score each baguette with 1, 3, or 5 long lengthwise cuts at a 30° to 45° angle.
13. Transfer baguettes on parchment to the hot steel or stone. Carefully pour about 1 cup of water into the cast iron pan(s) and close the oven door.
14. Immediately reduce oven temperature to 245°C.
15. Bake 24 to 28 minutes, until very deep golden brown. For a crisper crust, turn off the oven, prop the door open about 2 inches, and dry the baguettes for 10 minutes. Cool on a rack.

## Notes

- Status: Imported, untested
- For best results, weigh the flour; the recipe was developed by metric weight.
- These overnight baguettes are best eaten fresh. Wrap loosely and store leftovers at room temperature for up to several days; freeze for longer storage.
