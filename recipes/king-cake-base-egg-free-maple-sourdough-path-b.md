# King Cake Base — Egg-Free Maple Sourdough (Path B)

**Yield / Target**

* 1 king cake
* Dough weight: ~1,005-1,015 g (depending on dry milk)

## Formula (Baker's %)

* Total flour (incl starter flour): 100% (550 g)
* AP flour: 90.9% (500 g)
* Whole milk: 50% (275 g)
* Starter (100% hydration): 18.2% (100 g)
* Maple syrup: 10.9% (60 g)
* Butter, melted: 6.4% (35 g)
* Shortening: 4.5% (25 g)
* Salt: 1.8% (10 g)
* Nonfat dry milk (optional): 1.8% (10 g)
* Approx hydration (milk + starter water): ~59%

## Ingredients

* All-purpose flour: 500 g
* Whole milk: 275 g
* Sourdough starter (100% hydration): 100 g
* Fine salt: 10 g
* Unsalted butter, melted: 35 g
* Shortening: 25 g
* Pure maple syrup: 60 g
* Nonfat dry milk (optional): 10 g

## Process

1. **Mix**: Warm milk to 95-100F. Whisk in maple syrup and starter until fully combined. Add flour, salt, and nonfat dry milk (if using); mix until cohesive.
2. **Knead**: Knead 5-7 minutes (stand mixer, medium-low) until smooth, elastic, and moderately developed.
3. **Add fats**: Add melted butter slowly while mixing, then add shortening in small pieces. Knead 3-4 minutes more until fully incorporated and dough is silky.
4. **Bulk ferment**: Ferment at 72-74F for 90-120 minutes, targeting 60-75% rise (do not over-bulk). Optional cold bulk: refrigerate 8-14 hours after about 30-40% rise.
5. **Chill (if not cold bulked)**: Refrigerate 30-60 minutes before shaping to improve rope definition.
6. **Shape**: Roll dough, add filling, form ropes, twist/braid, and shape into a ring. Seal seams firmly.
7. **Final proof**: Proof at 75-78F for 60-90 minutes, until visibly puffy and jiggly.
8. **Bake**: Bake at 350F (177C) for 25-30 minutes, until evenly golden and internal temp reaches about 190F. Cool completely before icing or glazing.
