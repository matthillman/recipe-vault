# Soft and Moist Mardi Gras King Cake

**Yield / Target**

- 1 large king cake, 14–16 slices

## Ingredients

- whole milk: 177 ml, warm (38–43°C)
- honey: 59 ml
- instant yeast: 2 1/4 tsp (7 g)
- eggs, at room temperature: 4 large
- all-purpose flour: 4 1/2 cups (568 g)
- kosher salt: 2 tsp
- unsalted butter, softened to room temperature and cut into 1-inch pieces: 10 tbsp (1 1/4 sticks)
- dark or light brown sugar: 1 cup (110 g)
- ground cinnamon: 1 tbsp
- salted butter, melted: 2 tbsp
- heavy cream: 30 ml
- unsalted butter: 6 tbsp (3/4 stick)
- cream cheese, softened to room temperature: 113 g (1/2 block)
- confectioners' sugar, packed: 2 cups
- whole milk: 12 ml
- heavy cream, for brushing after baking: 59 ml

## Process

1. Heat the whole milk to 38–43°C. Whisk in the honey and instant yeast. Let stand for 10 minutes, until foamy. Whisk in the room-temperature eggs.
2. Combine the flour and kosher salt in a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment. On low speed, add the milk-and-egg mixture and mix until combined. Add the softened butter one piece at a time. Increase to medium speed and beat for 1 minute, until the butter is incorporated and the dough is stringy, sticky, and wet.
3. Transfer the dough to a large greased bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and let rise for 30 minutes.
4. Fold the dough by lifting one edge and folding it over itself. Repeat around the bowl about 6 times. Cover and let rise for 30 minutes. Repeat the folding step 3 more times, allowing 30 minutes between folds, for 2 hours and 4 folds total. Cover and refrigerate overnight or up to 72 hours.
5. For the icing, melt the 6 tbsp unsalted butter in a saucepan over medium heat for about 10 minutes, until lightly browned. Pour into a heat-safe bowl, cover, and let solidify overnight at room temperature.
6. Line an 18 x 13-inch baking sheet with parchment paper. Mix the brown sugar, cinnamon, and kosher salt in a small bowl.
7. Punch down the cold dough and knead about 10 times. On a floured surface, roll it into a 14 x 18-inch rectangle. Poke holes throughout with a fork about every 2 inches. Brush with the heavy cream and melted salted butter. Sprinkle with the cinnamon mixture and press lightly to adhere.
8. Starting from each long edge, roll the dough tightly toward the middle to create two logs in a scroll shape.
9. Transfer the dough to the lined baking sheet with the scroll side down. Form it into a U-shape, or pinch the ends together to make an oval. Stretch the thicker end as needed to make the shape more even.
10. Cut 1.5-inch slits about 1.5 inches apart around the cake's perimeter. Cut an additional slit in the thickest center section. Cover loosely and let rise in a warm spot for 1 to 1 1/2 hours.
11. Beat the solidified browned butter and cream cheese in a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment. Add the confectioners' sugar and whole milk, and mix just until combined. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
12. Bake for 32–35 minutes, rotating the pan halfway through, until light brown on top. While still warm, brush the cake all over with the heavy cream. Let it absorb for 15 minutes.
13. Cool completely, then spread with the brown-butter icing. Decorate with purple, green, and gold sanding sugar or sprinkles. Slice and serve.
