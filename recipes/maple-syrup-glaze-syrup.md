# Maple Syrup Glaze / Syrup

**Yield / Target**

* Enough to glaze 1 king cake or one medium loaf
* Thin for a syrup, thicker for a white-style drizzle glaze

## Ingredients

* Powdered sugar: 120 g
* Pure maple syrup: 45 g
* Plant milk or water (optional): 5-15 g

## Process

1. Warm maple syrup slightly.
2. Whisk into powdered sugar to desired thickness.
