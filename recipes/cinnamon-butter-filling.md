# Cinnamon Butter Filling

**Yield / Target**

* Enough for 1 king cake or one large filled loaf
* Adjust sugar and cinnamon by taste and dough size

## Ingredients

* Unsalted butter, soft: 113 g
* Brown sugar: 120 g
* Ground cinnamon: 8 g
* Fine salt: pinch

## Process

1. Cream all ingredients until fully spreadable.
2. Adjust cinnamon and sugar to taste.
