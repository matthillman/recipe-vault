# Yeast Dough for Kipferl

**Yield / Target**

- 12 pieces at 80 g each

## Ingredients

**Wheat Preferment (Dampfl)**

- Wheat flour, type 700: 125 g
- Milk (22–24 °C): 125 g
- Yeast: 20 g

**Main Dough**

- Wheat flour, type 480: 370 g
- Whole egg: 100 g
- Egg yolk: 40 g
- Butter: 75 g
- Sugar: 60 g
- Heavy cream: 40 g
- Honey: 20 g
- Salt: 7 g
- STROH rum: 12 g

## Process

1. For the preferment, combine all ingredients in a bowl, mix thoroughly, cover, and let mature at room temperature for 1 hour.
2. Combine the mature preferment with all main-dough ingredients except the butter and salt. Mix on low speed for 7–9 minutes.
3. Add the butter and salt, increase the speed slightly, and knead to a smooth dough. If kneading by hand, knead for 16–20 minutes.
4. Cover the dough and let it rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.
5. Transfer the dough to a work surface dusted with wheat flour, divide it, and round the portions into balls.
6. Cover the dough portions and let them relax for 15 minutes. Roll them into elongated shapes, let them rest briefly, and shape them into Kipferl.
7. Place the shaped dough on a parchment-lined baking sheet, brush with egg, and proof in a warm place for 20–25 minutes.
8. Brush the dough with egg a second time immediately before baking.
9. Bake in a preheated oven at 195 °C for 12–13 minutes, until golden brown.
10. Cool the baked Kipferl on a wire rack.
