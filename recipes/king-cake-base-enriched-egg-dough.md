# King Cake Base — Enriched Egg Dough

**Yield / Target**

* 1 king cake
* Dough weight: ~990 g

## Formula (Baker's %)

* Total flour: 100% (500 g)
* Whole milk: 40% (200 g)
* Eggs: 20% (100 g)
* Sugar: 12% (60 g)
* Unsalted butter: 22.6% (113 g)
* Salt: 2% (10 g)
* Instant yeast: 1.4% (7 g)

## Ingredients

* All-purpose flour: 500 g
* Whole milk: 200 g
* Instant yeast: 7 g
* Fine salt: 10 g
* Eggs: 100 g (about 2 large)
* Granulated sugar: 60 g
* Unsalted butter, very soft: 113 g (1 stick)

## Process

1. **Mix**: Warm milk to 95-100F. Whisk in yeast until dissolved. Add eggs and sugar; whisk smooth. Add flour and salt; mix until cohesive and slightly shaggy.
2. **Knead**: Knead 4-6 minutes (stand mixer, medium-low) to moderate gluten development. Dough should be soft, elastic, and slightly tacky (not sticky).
3. **Add butter**: Add butter gradually in small pieces; knead 3-5 minutes more until fully absorbed and dough is smooth, extensible, and glossy.
4. **Bulk ferment**: Ferment at 72-75F for 60-90 minutes, until roughly doubled.
5. **Chill (recommended)**: Refrigerate 30-60 minutes to firm for rolling and shaping.
6. **Shape**: Roll into a rectangle, add filling, form ropes, twist/braid, and shape into a ring. Seal seams firmly.
7. **Final proof**: Proof at 75-78F for 45-75 minutes, until puffy and lightly jiggly.
8. **Bake**: Bake at 350F (177C) for 25-30 minutes, until golden and internal temp reaches about 190F. Cool fully before icing.
