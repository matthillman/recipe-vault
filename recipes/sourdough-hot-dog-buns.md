# Sourdough Hot Dog Buns

**Yield / Target**

- 10 buns

## Ingredients

- whole milk, warmed to about 27°C: 180 g
- water: 100 g
- granulated sugar: 25 g
- instant yeast: 7 g
- bread flour: 500 g
- active sourdough starter: 100 g
- salt: 6 g
- butter, cubed and softened: 30 g

## Process

1. Combine the warm milk, warm water, sugar, and instant yeast in a stand mixer bowl. Mix on low speed until the sugar dissolves.
2. Add the sourdough starter, bread flour, and salt. Mix until a rough, shaggy dough forms.
3. Gradually add the softened butter and mix until the dough is smooth and elastic. Transfer to a greased bowl, cover, and rest until nearly doubled in size.
4. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
5. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and press it into a large rectangle. Cut into 10 pieces, about 90 g each.
6. Tuck each piece into a tight ball and place on the prepared baking sheet. Cover with plastic wrap and rest for 20 minutes.
7. Roll each ball into a rectangle about 6 inches wide. Roll each rectangle into a cylinder and pinch the seams shut.
8. Return the shaped buns to the baking sheet seam-side down, leaving a small gap between them. Cover with plastic wrap and let rise until nearly doubled.
9. Preheat the oven to 177°C. Brush each bun generously with milk.
10. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until the tops are golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 88°C.
11. Brush the buns with butter. Cool on the baking sheet before separating them.
