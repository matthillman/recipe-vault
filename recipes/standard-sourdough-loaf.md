# Standard Sourdough Loaf (75% Hydration, 20% Starter)

**Yield / Target**

* 1 medium boule or batard
* Finished dough weight: ~885 g

## Formula (Baker's %)

* Total flour: 100%
* Water: 75%
* Salt: 2%
* Starter (100% hydration): 20%

## Flour Blend (Total Flour = 500 g)

* Bread flour: 450 g (90%)
* Hard red wheat: 25 g (5%)
* Soft white wheat: 15 g (3%)
* Einkorn: 10 g (2%)

## Ingredients (Dough)

* Bread flour: 450 g
* Hard red wheat: 25 g
* Soft white wheat: 15 g
* Einkorn: 10 g
* Water: 325 g
* Salt: 10 g
* Active sourdough starter (100% hydration): 100 g

## Process

1. **Autolyse**: Mix flours and water (300 g) until no dry spots remain. Rest 30–45 minutes.
2. **Mix**: Add remaining water, starter and salt; mix until cohesive and moderately developed.
3. **Bulk Ferment**: 3–5 hours at 74–78°F, with 3–4 stretch-and-folds in the first 2 hours. Target ~40–60% rise and a slightly domed, jiggly dough.
4. **Pre-shape**: Lightly round; rest 20–30 minutes.
5. **Shape**: Final shape into boule or batard; place seam-side up in floured banneton.
6. **Cold Proof**: Refrigerate 12–18 hours at 38–40°F.
7. **Bake**: Preheat to 475°F with Dutch oven. Score and bake covered 20 minutes; uncover and finish 20–25 minutes to deep brown.
8. **Cool**: Rest at least 1 hour before slicing.

## Notes

* If your starter is less vigorous, extend bulk or proof slightly rather than pushing temperature.
* For a tighter crumb, reduce bulk rise toward ~40% and shorten final proof.
