# Pullman Sandwich Loaf

**Yield / Pan Target**

* USA Pan 9×4×4 Pullman (lid on)
* Finished dough weight: ~840 g

## Ingredients (USA Pan 9×4×4)

* Bread flour: 460 g
* Whole milk: 276 g
* Water: 19 g
* Unsalted butter: 37 g
* Honey: 18 g
* Salt: 9 g
* Sourdough starter (100% hydration): 69 g

## Process

1. Optional autolyse 20–30 minutes.
2. Mix all ingredients until moderately developed.
3. Bulk ferment 2.5–3.5 hours at ~74°F to 30–40% rise.
4. Shape and pan.
5. Proof until dough is ½–¾" below lid.
6. Bake covered at 375°F for 40–45 minutes.
