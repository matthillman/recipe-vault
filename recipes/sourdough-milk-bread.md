# Sourdough Milk Bread

**Yield / Target**

- 16
- one 9 in × 4 in loaf

## Ingredients

- lukewarm milk, preferably whole: 113 g
- lukewarm water: 28 g
- egg yolk (reserve the white for egg wash): 1 large
- unsalted butter, melted: 70 g
- granulated sugar: 24 g
- ripe sourdough starter: 250 g
- instant yeast: 6 g
- table salt: 9 g
- King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour: 300 g

## Process

1. Add all ingredients except the flour to the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the dough hook, or to a large mixing bowl, in the listed order. Weigh the flour, or measure it by spooning it into a cup and leveling off the excess; add it to the bowl.
2. Mix on low speed, or by hand with a bowl scraper, until the dough comes together.
3. Knead at medium-low speed, or on a lightly floured surface, until smooth, elastic, and tacky: 10 to 12 minutes in a stand mixer or up to 15 minutes by hand.
4. Shape the dough into a ball. Cover and rest it in a lightly greased bowl for 1 1/2 to 2 hours, until puffy and doubled in size.
5. Gently deflate the dough, transfer it to a lightly floured surface, and divide it into four equal pieces, about 200 g each.
6. Flatten the pieces. Roll each into a roughly 5 in × 8 in rectangle, with a short side nearest you.
7. Fold the long sides to meet in the middle and flatten to seal. Roll each rectangle from top to bottom into a tight tube.
8. Place the shaped pieces seam-side down in a greased 9 in × 4 in Extra-Tall Loaf Pan. Cover with plastic wrap or a reusable bowl cover.
9. Let rise in a warm spot for 2 to 3 hours, until the loaf reaches the top of the pan.
10. Near the end of the rise, preheat the oven to 191°C with a rack in the center position.
11. Gently brush the reserved egg white over the bread.
12. Bake for 36 to 40 minutes, until deep golden brown on top and slightly retracted from the pan edges. The internal temperature should be at least 96°C. Do not cover the loaf with the Pullman pan lid while baking.
13. Immediately turn the bread out of the pan and cool completely on a rack before slicing.

## Notes

- Status: Imported, untested
- Storage: Store leftover Sourdough Milk Bread well wrapped for up to 5 days; freeze for longer storage.
