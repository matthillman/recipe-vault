# Soft (Commercial-Style) Cheesesteak Rolls

**Yield / Target**

- 2 10-inch rolls

## Ingredients

- bread flour: 159 g
- water: 97 g
- instant dry yeast: 1.6 g (1/2 tsp)
- salt: 2.1 g
- Crisco: 7.1 g
- diastatic malt powder: 2.4 g (1 1/4 tsp)
- Scratch Dough Conditioner: 6.3 g (~1 Tbsp)

## Process

1. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl. Stir until thoroughly combined, cover, and rest for 15–20 minutes.
2. Knead for 3–5 minutes until smooth. Return dough to the bowl and let rise until 25–50% larger, about 30–60 minutes.
3. De-gas the dough, divide into 2 equal pieces, shape into balls, and place in spacious covered containers.
4. Refrigerate for 12–48 hours. For same-day baking, keep at room temperature instead.
5. Bring dough to room temperature; it should have grown to at least 2 times its size.
6. Flatten each dough ball with a rolling pin into a triangle about 1/2 inch thick.
7. Starting at the narrow end, roll into a log and seal the seam.
8. Roll from the center outward to shape each roll to about 9 inches long.
9. Sprinkle parchment with cornmeal, place rolls on it, cover with a slightly damp dish towel, and let rise.
10. Preheat the oven to 218°C.
11. Bake when rolls have about doubled in size and spring back slowly when poked.
12. Bake with steam for 12 minutes, then without steam for 4–6 minutes, rotating halfway through the uncovered period.
13. Cool completely for 30–60 minutes. Place in a plastic bag for at least 3–4 hours before cutting.

## Notes

- Status: Imported, untested
- Vegetable oil can be substituted for Crisco, but Crisco is described as ideal.
- The preferred steaming method is a Challenger Bread Pan.
- Import warning: Cornmeal is mentioned in the process but has no concrete numeric amount, so it was omitted from ingredients.
