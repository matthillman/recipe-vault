# New Orleans–Style French Bread (Po’boy)

**Yield / Pan Target**

* 2 long loaves (~14–16" each)
* Baked on sheet pan

## Ingredients (Baker’s %)

* Bleached AP flour: 100%
* Water: ~60%
* Instant yeast: ~1%
* Salt: ~2%
* Oil or shortening: ~3–4%

## Process

1. Mix until smooth and moderately developed.
2. Short bulk 60–90 minutes.
3. Divide, rest briefly, shape long loaves.
4. Proof until slightly puffy.
5. Bake hot on sheet pan with steam for pale, thin crust.
