# I. Base Recipes (Dialed / Production-Ready)

## Overnight Sourdough Focaccia

**Yield / Pan Target**

* Fits one standard 9×13" pan or equivalent sheet pan
* Finished dough weight: ~1,040 g

### Ingredients

* Bread flour: 450 g (90%)
* Whole wheat flour: 50 g (10%)
* Water: 410 g (82%)
* Active sourdough starter (100% hydration): 100 g (20%)
* Salt: 10 g (2%)
* Olive oil: 20 g (4%)

### Process

1. Autolyse flour and water 30–45 minutes.
2. Mix in starter. Add olive oil and salt; mix until cohesive.
3. Bulk ferment with 4 coil folds every 30–40 minutes.
4. Proof 3–5 hours at room temp **or** cold-retard overnight.
5. Transfer to oiled pan, stretch gently, dimple, oil generously.
6. Bake at 450°F (232°C) for 30–40 minutes until deeply golden.

## Brown Bread Sourdough

**Yield / Pan Target**

* One standard Pullman or 9×5" loaf pan
* Finished dough weight: ~835 g

### Ingredients

**Levain**

* Starter: 20 g
* Water: 70 g
* Bread flour: 70 g

**Dough**

* Bread flour: 270 g (90%)
* Whole wheat flour: 30 g (10%)
* Cocoa powder: 3 g
* Water: 180 g (+ up to 20 g if needed)
* Levain: 150 g
* Honey: 78 g
* Sugar: 9 g
* Salt: 6 g
* Soft butter: 30 g
* Old-fashioned oats (for coating): ~½ cup

### Process

1. Build levain and ferment overnight.
2. Mix all dough ingredients except butter until cohesive; add butter and knead smooth.
3. Bulk ferment 5–6 hours at 76–80°F with 3 stretch-and-folds.
4. Shape loaf; roll exterior in oats.
5. Short room-temp proof, then cold-proof 14–24 hours.
6. Bake covered at 500°F for 15 minutes; uncover and finish at 450°F ~20 minutes.

## Hybrid Sourdough Demi-Baguettes

**Yield / Pan Target**

* 5 demi-baguettes, ~170 g each
* Baked directly on stone or steel

### Ingredients (5 × 170 g loaves)

* Total flour: 514 g

  * 70% Bread flour
  * 20% AP flour
  * 10% Whole wheat flour
* Water: 377 g (73.5%)
* Salt: 10.3 g (2%)
* Sourdough starter (100% hydration): 52 g (10%)
* Instant yeast: 0.6 g (0.12%)

### Process

1. Autolyse flour + most water 30–45 minutes.
2. Mix in starter, yeast, salt, remaining water.
3. Warm bulk 60–75 minutes with one fold.
4. Cold bulk 10–14 hours at 38–40°F targeting ~50–60% rise.
5. Divide cold, pre-shape, rest 20–30 minutes.
6. Final shape; proof 60–90 minutes at 75–78°F.
7. Bake 475–485°F with steam for 22–25 minutes.

## Default Pullman Sandwich Loaf

**Yield / Pan Target**

* USA Pan 9×4×4 Pullman (lid on)
* Finished dough weight: ~840 g

### Ingredients (USA Pan 9×4×4)

* Bread flour: 460 g
* Whole milk: 276 g
* Water: 19 g
* Unsalted butter: 37 g
* Honey: 18 g
* Salt: 9 g
* Sourdough starter (100% hydration): 69 g

### Process

1. Optional autolyse 20–30 minutes.
2. Mix all ingredients until moderately developed.
3. Bulk ferment 2.5–3.5 hours at ~74°F to 30–40% rise.
4. Shape and pan.
5. Proof until dough is ½–¾" below lid.
6. Bake covered at 375°F for 40–45 minutes.

## Yeasted Soft Pretzels

**Yield / Pan Target**

* 8 large soft pretzels
* Standard sheet pan lined with parchment

### Ingredients

* Warm water (95–105°F): 355 g
* Sugar: 1 Tbsp
* Kosher salt: 6 g
* Instant yeast: 5.25 g
* All-purpose flour: 630 g
* Unsalted butter, melted: 56 g

**Boiling Bath**

* Water: 10 cups
* Baking soda: 190 g

**Egg Wash**

* Egg yolk: 17 g
* Water: 1 Tbsp

### Process

1. Mix and knead 4–5 minutes.
2. Bulk ferment 45–55 minutes until doubled.
3. Divide into 8; roll 24" ropes; shape.
4. Boil 30 seconds per pretzel.
5. Egg wash, salt, bake at 450°F for 12–14 minutes.

## New Orleans–Style French Bread (Po’boy)

**Yield / Pan Target**

* 2 long loaves (~14–16" each)
* Baked on sheet pan

### Ingredients (Baker’s %)

* Bleached AP flour: 100%
* Water: ~60%
* Instant yeast: ~1%
* Salt: ~2%
* Oil or shortening: ~3–4%

### Process

1. Mix until smooth and moderately developed.
2. Short bulk 60–90 minutes.
3. Divide, rest briefly, shape long loaves.
4. Proof until slightly puffy.
5. Bake hot on sheet pan with steam for pale, thin crust.

# II. Modular Fillings & Components

## Almond Cream Filling (Frangipane-Style)

### Ingredients

* Cream cheese, very soft: 100 g
* Unsalted butter, very soft: 100 g
* Granulated sugar: 100 g
* Almond flour: 100 g
* Egg: 50 g
* Almond extract: 3 g
* Vanilla extract: 2 g
* AP flour (stabilizer): 10 g

### Process

1. Cream butter, cream cheese, and sugar until smooth.
2. Mix in egg, extracts, then almond flour and AP flour.
3. Use immediately or refrigerate up to 3 days.

# III. Reference Frameworks & Techniques

## Overnight Sourdough Waffles (King Arthur Reference)

### Ingredients

**Overnight Sponge**

* Sourdough discard: 227 g
* Buttermilk: 454 g
* AP flour: 240 g
* Sugar: 28 g

**Morning Additions**

* Eggs: 2
* Oil 50 g **or** butter 57 g
* Baking soda: 1 tsp
* Salt: ¾ tsp

### Process

1. Mix sponge; rest ~12 hours at cool room temp.
2. Whisk in remaining ingredients.
3. Cook until deeply golden.

---

## Waffle Crisp Mode (Framework)

* Replace 10–15% flour with cornstarch
* Use oil or clarified butter
* Cook fully, then vent briefly before serving

---

## Sourdough Framework (The Bread Code)

* Starter % = primary fermentation throttle
* Temperature = speed multiplier
* Hydration = sensitivity control
* Enzyme load = silent accelerator
* Stop bulk based on strength, not clock time

---

## Ruhlman Ratios (Reference)

* Bread: 5 flour : 3 water
* Pasta: 3 flour : 2 egg
* Pie dough: 3 flour : 2 fat : 1 water
* Muffins: 2 flour : 2 liquid : 1 egg : 1 fat
* Vinaigrette: 3 oil : 1 acid
