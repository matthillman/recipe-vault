# Cocktail Protocol: Clarified Nitro Virgin Piña Colada

## Summary

A service-ready, **clear** piña colada build (pineapple + coconut + lime) designed to be stable enough for **nitro** service. The core trick is **milk clarification**, which strips haze, oils, and some harsh edges.

## Safety / Risks

- **Do not pressurize glass bottles** with N2/CO2 unless you have rated packaging and process controls. Prefer kegging or a purpose-built nitro bottle system.
- Dairy allergen (whole milk).

## Targets

- Very low particulate (to avoid nucleation/clogs and to get a stable nitro cascade).
- Stable clarity after 24 hours cold.

## Ingredients (Starting Point, 1 Batch)

Punch base (adjust to taste):

- Pineapple juice: 600 g
- Coco Lopez: 250 g
- Lime juice: 120 g
- Water: 30 g (optional; use to adjust sweetness/acid)
- Salt: 1 g

Clarifier:

- Whole milk: **20–25% of punch-base weight** (example: 200–250 g milk per 1,000 g punch base)

Optional (high leverage for filter speed / stability):

- Pectinase enzyme (brand-specific dosing; see `notes/science/clarification/pectinase-clarification.md`)

## Procedure

1. **Pre-filter pineapple**: strain juice through a fine mesh to remove coarse pulp.
2. **(Optional) Pectinase**: treat the pineapple juice (or the full punch base) per manufacturer guidance; rest until it thins/settles.
3. **Build punch base**: whisk pineapple juice, Coco Lopez, lime, water, and salt until fully homogeneous.
4. **Cold crash (optional but helpful)**: chill 2–12 hours, then skim any surface oil and decant off heavy sediment.
5. **Milk clarification**:
   1. Put the **milk** in a large vessel.
   2. Slowly pour the **punch base into the milk** while stirring gently. Curds should form.
   3. Rest 30–60 minutes cold.
6. **Filter (staged)**:
   1. Strain through a fine mesh to catch large curds.
   2. Filter through a bag filter / cloth.
   3. Polish through coffee filters (multiple funnels in parallel helps a lot).
7. **Stability check**: chill 24 hours and re-check clarity. If haze returns, re-filter and/or add pectinase earlier next time.

## Nitro Service (No-Centrifuge Friendly)

Best options:

- **Keg + nitrogen**: chill hard, charge/condition on N2, and serve through a stout/nitro faucet (or a restrictor nozzle).
- **iSi nitro device** (if you have one): keep the clarified mix very cold; charge, shake, rest briefly, then dispense immediately.
- **NitroPress Pro**: best when the liquid is very cold, very low particulate, and not overly viscous (see `notes/equipment/nitropress-pro.md`).

Notes:

- Any remaining coconut oils or fines can collapse foam or clog restrictors; prioritize staged filtration.

### NitroPress Pro Tips (House Defaults)

1. Chill the clarified mix hard (fridge-cold at minimum).
2. If flow/cascade is weak, reduce viscosity by cutting Coco Lopez slightly and compensating sweetness/body with clearer components (water + sugar, or coconut water plus sugar).
3. Filter until you can chill 24 hours with no “snow globe” sediment; NitroPress performance is very sensitive to fines.

## Troubleshooting

- **Filters clog instantly**: too much pulp/pectin → pre-filter, cold-settle, use pectinase, then filter.
- **Not clearing**: insufficient curd formation → increase acidity slightly or increase milk % and rest longer before filtering.
- **Nitro looks “flat”**: mix is too warm, too viscous, or still oily/particulate → chill more, filter finer, consider reducing Coco Lopez slightly and compensating sweetness elsewhere.

## Logging

Record:

- Juice source (fresh/canned), pulp level
- Milk % used
- Filtration ladder (exact steps)
- Time-to-filter + yield
- Clarity immediately and after 24 hours cold
