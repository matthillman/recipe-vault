# Centrifuge Notes (Clarification)

## Summary

Centrifugation is the fastest “low-adulteration” path to clarity if you have the equipment. It separates based on density without adding fining agents.

## Practical Guidance

- Pre-filter coarse solids to avoid forming fluffy layers that remix during decanting.
- Spin, then **decant slowly**; stop before disturbing the pellet.
- For pectin-heavy juices, pectinase first can dramatically improve both clarity and ease of separation.

## Logging

Record:

- Relative centrifugal force (RCF / g), time, temperature
- Volume per tube and fill level
- Clarity immediately and after 24 hours cold
