# Protocol: Agar Clarification (Juice / Punch)

## Summary

Use a low % of agar to form a gel, then break it and filter. The gel traps particulates and some haze-formers. Great when you don’t have a centrifuge.

## Safety / Risks

- Heat + hot sugar/acid liquids can burn; handle hot liquids carefully.
- If clarifying alcoholic punches, avoid boiling off alcohol: heat only as needed to dissolve agar.

## Inputs

- Base liquid: juice or pre-mixed punch
- Agar agar: start at **0.20–0.35%** by total liquid weight (bench test)
- Optional: pectinase first for pectin-heavy juices (see `notes/science/clarification/pectinase-clarification.md`)

Equipment:

- Scale, saucepan
- Immersion blender (helpful)
- Container for setting gel
- Fine filter: nut milk bag / Superbag / multiple coffee filters / fine chinois + filter

## Procedure

1. **Pre-filter (optional but recommended):** strain through a fine chinois to remove large pulp.
2. **Hydrate agar:** whisk agar into cold liquid (or a small portion of it) to avoid clumps.
3. **Heat to activate:** bring to a full simmer while whisking; hold ~30–60 seconds (enough to fully dissolve).
4. **Set gel:** pour into a shallow container; chill until fully set.
5. **Break gel:** cut into cubes, then gently whisk or briefly blend to form a loose gel slurry.
6. **Filter:** pour into your filter setup and let drain under gravity (don’t squeeze). Re-filter if needed.
7. **Cold hold:** chill clarified liquid. If haze returns over time, treat pectin (enzyme) and/or filter finer.

## Measurements / Targets

- Visual clarity: readable text through 1–2 cm in a clear glass is a practical target.
- Yield: expect meaningful loss; record % yield for your setup.

## Troubleshooting

- **Still hazy:** agar dose too low, gel not fully activated, or pectin haze not addressed → increase agar slightly and/or use pectinase.
- **Slow/no filtration:** filter too fine for heavy solids → pre-filter; let settle first; use a staged filtration (coarse then fine).
- **Flavor muted:** agar can strip some aromatic compounds → use enzyme + filtration instead when aroma is priority.

## Variations

- **Two-pass:** agar clarify, then a second pass through coffee filters for “cocktail-clear.”
- **Cold-crash first:** chill and settle overnight, decant, then agar clarify for better yield.
