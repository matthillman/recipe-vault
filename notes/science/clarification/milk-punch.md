# Protocol: Milk Punch Clarification (Batch Cocktails)

## Summary

Milk punch clarifies by forming curds (casein network) that trap particles and some tannins, producing a clear, shelf-friendlier cocktail with a rounder profile.

## Safety / Risks

- Allergens: dairy.
- Food safety: keep cold; if storing, treat as perishable unless you’ve validated shelf stability.
- Acids + dairy can foam/overflow; add slowly.

## Inputs

- Finished cocktail base (spirits + sugar + acids + tea/juice as desired)
- Whole milk (or milk alternative if specifically tested; whole milk is the baseline)
- Acid source: citrus juice and/or added acid (citric/malic) depending on recipe

Equipment:

- Large container, whisk
- Fine strainer + coffee filters (or Superbag)

## Procedure (General)

1. **Build the punch** (excluding milk). Chill if possible.
2. **Add milk to vessel** first.
3. **Add punch to milk slowly** while stirring gently (not the other way around). Curds should form.
4. Rest 30–60 minutes cold to let curds firm up.
5. **Filter** through a fine strainer to catch large curds, then through coffee filters. Re-filter until clear.
6. Bottle and hold cold. Record clarity over time (haze reappearance is a signal to adjust acid/milk ratio or filtration).

## Measurements / Targets

- The “right” milk % depends on acidity and composition. Start with bench tests.
- Practical target: stable clarity after 24–48 hours in the fridge.

## Troubleshooting

- **No curds:** insufficient acidity → increase acid or use more citrus.
- **Not clearing:** curds too fine / agitation too high → stir gently; allow longer rest; use staged filtration.
- **Flavor too “soft”:** milk punch rounds edges; for a brighter result, use enzyme + filtration instead.

## Notes

This is the most “transformative” clarification method in this folder; it’s not a neutral clarification.
