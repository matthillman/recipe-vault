# Further Clarification Techniques To Consider

These are useful additions beyond agar/gel clarification and milk punch. Bench-test before committing a full batch.

## Techniques

- **Citrus “oil management”**
  - Cold crash, skim surface oils, and avoid heavy peel inclusion (oils can create persistent haze and filtration issues).
- **Freeze concentration + decant**
  - Partial-freeze juice, collect the early melt fraction for clearer, more concentrated liquid (changes flavor and balance).
- **Fining agents (wine-style)**
  - Bentonite (often targets protein haze), PVPP (polyphenols), gelatin/isinglass (some tannins), kieselsol/chitosan two-part finings.
  - These are powerful but can be ingredient- and allergen-sensitive; document brand/dose and do bench trials.
- **Activated carbon**
  - Can strip off-flavors and color; easy to overdo. Use only when needed and test at small scale.
- **Plate/membrane filtration**
  - Very effective for “production” clarity; requires equipment and a good pre-filtration strategy.

## What To Document When We Try These

- Liquid type (juice/punch; pH; sugar; alcohol % if relevant)
- Dose, temperature, time
- Clarity after 0h/24h/1 week
- Flavor/aroma impact
- Yield and process pain points (clogging, slow filtration, separation)
