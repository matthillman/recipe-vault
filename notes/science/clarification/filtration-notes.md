# Filtration Notes (For Clear Juices & Cocktails)

## Summary

Filtration works best as a staged process: remove big stuff first, then go finer. Many “it won’t filter” problems are really “we skipped a coarse step.”

## Practical Filtration Ladder

1. **Settle + decant** (cold hold overnight if possible).
2. **Coarse strain**: chinois / mesh strainer.
3. **Bag filter**: nut milk bag / Superbag.
4. **Paper**: coffee filters (slow, but can get very clear).
5. **Fine membrane** (advanced): 5 micron → 1 micron (requires appropriate hardware).

## Tips

- Don’t squeeze filters if you want clarity; squeezing pushes fines through.
- Paper filters clog quickly with pectin/pulp; treat pectin (enzyme) or pre-filter first.
- Cold liquids filter slower but may keep more aroma; warm slightly only if needed.

## For Bottling / Nitro / Carbonation

- Aim for extremely low particulate to reduce nucleation/clogging.
- Prefer enzyme + staged filtration; consider membrane filtration if you have the setup.
